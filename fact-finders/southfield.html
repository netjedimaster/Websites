Your fix plan

Five steps to put Southfield on the map

  1. Create and verify the Southfield Google listing

    Correct categories (Security guard service, Private investigator, Process server), hours, a local phone number and real photos. Google verifies by postcard or video at the address, so someone needs to be able to receive it there. If nobody works at that address day to day, list it as a service-area business with the address hidden instead, so the listing doesn't get suspended.

    Seen +16

  2. Make your name unmistakable

    Use the same name everywhere, "Fact Finders Group, Inc." with your logo and 28 years, so nobody confuses you with Fact Finders Polygraph Services.

    Seen +3

  3. Add a Southfield page to your website

    What you do in Metro Detroit, the local phone number, a quote form and a text button, linked from the listing.

    Chosen +12

  4. Start reviews on day one

    A text after every job from this office asking for a Google review. Your Chicago clients can't review this listing; it needs its own.

    Trusted +20

  5. Post weekly and answer every review

    Photos of officers on post, case wins you can share, and a reply to every review within a day.

    Trusted +8

All five together move this office from 4 to about 63, into Competitive, within the first year. Map spots follow as reviews build up. We track your rankings every two weeks.

Rules we follow: no gifts or discounts for reviews (Google and FTC rules). Review texts run on a registered (A2P) number and go only to clients who expect them, never to confidential investigation clients.
