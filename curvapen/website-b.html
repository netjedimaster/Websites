Our story

It started as a better pen. Then my mother had a stroke.

I'm Clarence Parker, an inventor in Charlotte, NC. CurvaPen began as a better way to write for everyone, and over $1 million in sales later, artists, architects and lefties had found it.

Then a stroke took the control of my mother's right side. She took pride in her handwriting. She picked up a CurvaPen, wrote her name, and smiled. Her occupational therapists asked one question: where can we get this for our other patients?

Our mission is simple: to help every hand reclaim its independence through the power of handwriting.

Questions

Common questions

Will this fix my handwriting?

No pen can promise that. CurvaPen is designed to make writing easier to see and easier to guide, from almost any grip. CurvaPen is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

Will it help with tremors?

It won't stop a tremor. Its forgiving nib keeps ink flowing and the tip in view, which many people with tremors find easier to manage. The Starter Pack is a low-cost way to find out.

Can I use it left-handed or overhand?

Yes. It's fully ambidextrous and works overhand, underhand or sideways.

Is it weighted?

No. It's lightweight and balanced. If you want more to hold, add the wide gripper at checkout.

Where can I buy it?

Here on curvapen.com, and on Walmart.com. Clinics and schools can request bulk pricing and sample kits above.

What if it doesn't work for me?
