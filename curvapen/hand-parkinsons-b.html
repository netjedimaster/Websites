Tried a weighted pen?

Extra weight isn't the only answer.

Some people find weighted pens helpful. Many find a heavy pen tires the hand sooner, and it still leaves two problems: you can't see the tip, and you're stuck at one cramped angle.

Simple ways to make writing easier

Write during your "on" time

Save cards and forms for when medication works best.

Think big

Aim for larger letters. Wide-ruled paper gives you a target.

Support your arm

Forearm on the table. A steady base means a steadier hand.

Keep the paper still

A clipboard, a non-slip mat, or a little tape.

Go short and rest

A few lines, then a break.

Print if it's easier

Clear block letters are just as much yours.

Questions

Common questions

How is it different from a normal pen?

The tip is curved. Your hand stops blocking your view of the line, and you can hold it overhand, underhand or sideways and the nib still meets the page.

Can I use it left-handed?

Yes. It's fully ambidextrous, and the quick-drying ink doesn't drag under your hand.

Is it weighted?

No. It's light and balanced. If you want more to hold, add the Fat Gripper for $5 in your cart.

Will it help with tremors or after a stroke?

It won't stop a tremor. Many people find the page easier to manage because they can see the tip and rest their hand. CurvaPen is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

Where can I buy it?

Here on curvapen.com, and on Walmart.com. Clinics, schools and stores can ask for bulk and wholesale pricing.

What if it doesn't work for me?
