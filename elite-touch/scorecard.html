Two things to settle before Google will list you

Your LLC is in South Carolina

Elite Touch Services LLC is on file in South Carolina with a Greenville address. To do business in North Carolina, an out-of-state LLC usually registers with the NC Secretary of State, or the owner forms a new NC LLC. Google can ask for business documents when it verifies a listing, so your name and address need to match. Your accountant or attorney can tell you which route fits, and we build everything around it.

Someone here already uses "Elite Touch"

Elite Touch Cleaning Services on England St in Charlotte has 89 reviews, a 4.9 and 15+ years in business. People around Concord and Charlotte who hear "Elite Touch" from a happy client and search for it land on them, and Google may treat a second Elite Touch as a duplicate. Worth a quick talk with an attorney about the name before anything is printed. A small, distinctive change makes you easy to find and impossible to confuse.
