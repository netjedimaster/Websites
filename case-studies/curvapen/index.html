What we built

Six pieces, one system

A store that's easy to buy from

Home page for everyone, six "shop by hand" pages, and three packs instead of a confusing menu. Every buy button goes straight to the color picker. Free U.S. shipping over $20.

Proof people can see

16 customer videos, branded with captions and names, plus 62 product photos and the founder's own story. Each video sits on the page of the buyer it speaks to.

Gifts that grow the order

A free guide with every order and three holiday workbooks with every bundle. A free handwriting guide on the Parkinson's page builds the email list.

Reviews on autopilot

Google sends every buyer a short survey after delivery. Day 7 and Day 13 emails remind them. Past buyers get a personal note from the founder asking for a Facebook review.

Follow-up that sells

Sign-up offer, abandoned cart, how-to video, reorder reminders, win-back for old customers and comment-to-DM on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Google, Facebook and the inbox

Google Shopping listings, sales tracking with real dollar values, old agency access removed from Facebook, carrier texting registration and a dedicated sending address.

Less friction, more orders

One tap to the pack you want

Most of CurvaPen's buyers shop on a phone, and many are older or have hands that don't cooperate. So we made buying simple.

  • Three packs named by what you get: Starter 2-Pack, Everyday 6-Pack, Classroom & Clinic 20-Pack
  • Mix colors inside a bundle with big plus and minus buttons
  • Free gifts shown right on the pack, so the bundle is the easy choice
  • No pop-up in your face on phones. The offer waits in the bottom bar
  • Tested on a slowed-down phone so early taps never land on the wrong page
Real customers, real words

Proof that sells better than any ad

CurvaPen's customers had already filmed themselves. We branded the videos and put each one where the right buyer would see it. Tap to play.
