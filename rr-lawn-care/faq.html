Questions and answers

What you'll probably want to know

What are the next steps if I say yes?

Five steps, and most of them take minutes.
1. Say yes. Reply YES to Phyllena by text or email.
2. Sign a one-page agreement. We send it by email and you sign on your phone.
3. First month. You pay the first month ($497) through a secure payment link. The setup fee is waived.
4. A 30-minute kickoff call. We confirm the business name you'll file in Florida, get your legal name and EIN (the phone carriers require those to approve your texting number), your average job price, the towns you want work in, and your customer list.
5. We start. Week one begins the next business day: your Google listing submitted, your domain registered in your name and your website started.

I don't have a registered business yet. Is that a problem?

No, it means we start fresh, which is easier than fixing a messy listing. Google can ask for business documents when it verifies a listing, and the phone carriers need a business name to approve your texting number, so step one is filing your name in Florida on Sunbiz. A fictitious name (DBA) runs about $50; an LLC keeps the lawn work separate from your trucking. Your accountant can tell you which fits. Adding your town, like "R&R Lawn Care of Hollywood," keeps you from being confused with the other R&R lawn companies in Florida. Once the name is filed, we build everything on it.

How long does setup take?

About 2 to 4 weeks for everything. Google verification usually takes a few days to two weeks. Your website goes live in weeks two to four. Review and list texts start once the phone carriers approve your texting number, which takes about two weeks.

How soon will I see results?

Some right away, some over a few months. Your listing and website: weeks two to four. Reviews: about 10 from your current customers in the first month, then about 5 a month. Spots in the map results usually start showing in months two and three and keep building. You'll see all of it in your monthly report.

Are there any outside costs?

From us, no. Everything is in the $497: the website and hosting, your domain, texting fees, the texting number registration, review replies, your Google listing, town pages and your monthly report. We don't run ads, so there's no ad budget. The Florida filing fee for your business name is paid to the state, not to us.

What do I have to do?

About 20 seconds per job. When a job is done, tap the "Job done" button saved on your phone and type the customer's first name and cell. Add a before-and-after photo of the yard if you like and we post it to Google. Storm coming or palms due? Tap "Send a special" and tell us what you want to offer. We write the text and send it the same day.

Will my customers get annoyed by texts?

Only customers who say yes to your list get seasonal texts, about 2 a month, with its own checkbox or "Reply JOIN." Every text says "Reply STOP to opt out," and anyone who does is removed automatically. Your texting number is registered with the phone carriers, as the law requires.

Can I give a discount for leaving a review?

No. Google and FTC rules don't allow rewards for reviews, and it can get reviews removed. Google does allow asking every customer for a review, and that's what we do, the same way, every time.

Can you delete bad reviews?

Nobody can delete a real review, and anyone who promises that is a risk to your listing. We can report reviews that break Google's rules, like fake or off-topic ones, and Google decides. What works is answering every review calmly and getting enough fresh, real reviews. Any review under 4 stars alerts you right away, and a real person writes the reply with you.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee where Google ranks you. We guarantee the work: your listing and website built and kept up, a thank-you text after every job, every review answered, your list texts sent, and a monthly report showing exactly where you stand.

Who owns my Google listing, website and customer list?

You do. You're the owner of your Google listing and we're added as managers. Your domain is registered in your name. Your website and customer list are yours, and if you ever leave, we hand them over at no charge.

Is there a contract?

A simple one-page service agreement, month to month. No long-term commitment. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

Why is the setup fee waived?

We're building our first group of founding clients. In exchange for the waived $1,500 setup, we ask for a testimonial once you see results, permission to share your results as a case study, and an introduction to anyone you know who could use the same help.

How does billing work?

One flat charge of $497 a month, billed automatically on the same day each month, starting the day you sign. That's your founder price, regularly $997, locked in for as long as you stay. No per-text charges and no surprise add-ons.

What if I hire a crew or add another area?

That's the goal. More towns, like Cooper City, Weston or Fort Lauderdale, get their own pages at no extra cost. A second office with its own address gets its own Google listing for $297 a month more. We'll quote it when you're ready.

How will I know it's working?

Every month you get a plain-English report: where you rank on the 11 searches in your scorecard, how many new reviews came in, your star rating, how many customers are on your text list, and what we did that month.
