Two things to settle before Google will list you

Make the business official

Google can ask for business documents when it verifies a listing, and the texting system needs a registered business name too. In Florida that's a quick filing on Sunbiz: a fictitious name (DBA) for about $50, or an LLC if you want the lawn work kept separate from your trucking. Your accountant can tell you which fits. We build everything around the name you file.

"R&R Lawn" is a crowded name

Florida has more than a dozen R&R lawn companies on file, and one called R&R Lawn Care of South Florida already has a Google listing with a single 1-star review. A homeowner who hears "R&R" from a neighbor and searches for it could land on them. Adding your town, like "R&R Lawn Care of Hollywood," makes you easy to find and impossible to confuse.
