The five fixes that move your score the most

  1. Ship U.S. orders from the U.S.

    Keep a stock of the best sellers in Fort Mill. Delivery in days, U.S. exchanges, and "ships from South Carolina" right on the product page.

    Be Chosen

  2. Open a U.S. store and put it in Google Shopping, free

    The Kiques USA store we mocked up, with a U.S. product feed so every style shows in the Shopping box.

    Be Seen

  3. Turn your 78 reviews into stars on Google, and keep them coming

    Send your rating to Google and ask every U.S. buyer for a review by text and email after delivery.

    Be Trusted

  4. Build a page for each buyer

    Line dancers, weddings, concerts, the office, and "comfortable heels that feel like sneakers," plus a page for U.S. boutiques and dance groups.

    Be Seen

  5. Start a new-release and second-pair list

    Email and text sign-up with a reason to join, then drops, color reminders and try-on event invites.

    Be Chosen

Together these lift the score from 40 to about 76, near the top of Competitive, with rankings checked and reported every month.
