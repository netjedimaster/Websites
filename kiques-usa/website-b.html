Questions

Before you buy.

Are they really comfortable for a heel?

Yes. Every style has a cushioned foam or memory foam insole, a roomy sneaker toe box and laces that hold your foot in place. That's why every one of our 78 reviews is 5 stars, and many mention dancing in them.

How high is the heel?

8.5cm, about 3.3 inches, on every style.

What are they made of?

Synthetic vegan leather uppers (some styles add faux suede or faux nubuck), a foam and mesh or spandex lining, a rubber outsole, and a replaceable heel tip.

What if they don't fit?

Unworn pairs can be returned or exchanged for another size. Use the size guide above, and if you're between sizes, go up.

Who is Kiques USA?

Kiques was designed in Australia. Kiques USA is run by the brand's American co-founder, Tabithia, from Fort Mill, SC, just outside Charlotte, so US customers, boutiques and dance groups have a local team.
