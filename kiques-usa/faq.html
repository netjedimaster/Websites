Questions, answered

Everything you'd ask before you say yes.

What happens next?

Reply YES to Phyllena or book a 15-minute call. We send a short agreement and a setup checklist, get access to your Shopify store, and start week one. You keep running Kiques USA; we run the Google side.

How long does setup take?

2 to 4 weeks. The U.S. store, Google Shopping feed and review stars go first, usually inside two weeks. Buyer pages and the sign-up list follow.

When will I see results?

Google Shopping listings can start showing within days of the feed being approved. Regular search rankings build over 60 to 120 days. You'll get a ranking report every month so you can watch it move.

Do I need U.S. stock?

We strongly recommend it. Today a U.S. order can take up to 18 business days from Australia. Keeping your best sellers in Fort Mill turns that into days, and that's what makes a $270 heel an easy yes. We'll help you pick the starting sizes and styles from your sales.

Are there outside costs?

Your Shopify plan, any Google Shopping ads you choose to run (optional, and you set the budget), and text message fees once texting is turned on, usually a few dollars a month at your size. We'll tell you before anything costs money.

How much of my time does this take?

About an hour in week one for logins and a quick call, then a few minutes a month to look at your report. You keep doing what you're great at: meeting women and getting Kiques on their feet.

Who gets texted?

Only people who say yes. Buyers and shoppers check a separate box or reply JOIN. Before any text goes out we register your number with the carriers, which the law requires.

Can you delete bad reviews?

No one can delete honest reviews, and you don't need to: all 78 of yours are five stars. Every new review gets watched and answered, and we never offer discounts in exchange for reviews, because Google and the FTC don't allow it.

Does this follow Google's rules?

Yes. Real product data, real reviews, real pages. No tricks that put your store at risk.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No honest company can. We guarantee the work, the monthly report, and that you'll always see exactly where you stand.

Who owns the store and the list?

You do. The store, the product feed, the reviews and every customer on the list belong to Kiques USA.

Is there a long contract?

Month to month. Your $497 founder price stays locked in for as long as you stay.

What about Kaitee and the Australian store?

Nothing changes for Australia. We build the U.S. side with the same products, photos and prices, and we're happy to share every report with Kaitee.

Why is setup waived?

Setup is normally $1,500. We waive it for founding clients in exchange for a testimonial, a case study once results are in, and an introduction or two if you're happy.

What do the monthly reports show?

Where Kiques ranks on each buyer search, Shopping clicks, new reviews, list sign-ups and orders from Google, in plain English.
