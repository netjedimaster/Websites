The five fixes that move your score the most

  1. A phone-first website that shows what you carry

    Pages for vitamins, protein and sports nutrition, peptides, herbs and teas, sea moss and alkaline water, with your reviews, your team and a "Text us to hold it" button so shoppers can reserve a product and pick it up.

  2. Town pages and weekly Google posts

    Tell Google you serve Pineville, Matthews, Huntersville, Concord, Mooresville, Gastonia and Rock Hill, and that you carry protein, creatine, pre-workout and peptides.

    Seen +8

  3. Text every buyer: a review request and a VIP invite

    Staff enter a first name and cell at checkout (about 10 seconds). A thank-you text asks for a Google review and invites them to join your deal and restock list, with its own yes.

    Trusted +3

    Thank every reviewer by name, answer the cat-supplement shopper, and add photos of the aisles, the water station and new arrivals.

    Trusted +5

  5. Fix your Yelp name and add Google categories

    One name everywhere, so Apple Maps matches Google. Add health food and sports nutrition categories.

    Seen +4

All five together lift the score from 56 to about 88, into Local Authority. Rankings in nearby towns build over two to three months of steady reviews and posting, which we track and report every month.
