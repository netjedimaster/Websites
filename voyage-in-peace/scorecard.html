Your fix plan

The five fixes that move your score the most

  1. Create and verify a Google profile for Voyage in Peace Travel

    Home-based, service-area setup (address hidden) covering Concord, Kannapolis, Harrisburg and Charlotte, with travel agency categories, your trip types, hours and your website linked. Lead with "Voyage in Peace," not "VIP," so you're never confused with VIP Travel downtown.

    Seen +16

  2. Text every traveler: a review request and a list invite

    A personal text to past travelers now, then a "welcome home" text after every trip. It asks for a Google review and invites them onto your deals list.

    Trusted +20

  3. Build a site that's about you and your trips

    Your own domain with your story, girls' trip, cruise, all-inclusive, honeymoon, family and group pages, a "plan my trip" form, a text button and your reviews. Your InteleTravel booking tools stay connected.

    Chosen +12

  4. Add 30+ trip photos

    Your travelers' beach, cruise and group photos (with their OK), posted to Google every week.

    Trusted +5

  5. Business pages, one local number, one business email

    A Voyage in Peace Facebook and Instagram business page, a local 704 or 980 number for calls and texts, and a business email instead of Yahoo, the same everywhere.

    Seen +2

All five together lift the score from 9 to about 64, into Competitive. Map spots (worth up to 20 more points) follow as fresh reviews and trip pages build up. We send you a ranking report every month.
