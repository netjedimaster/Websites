  2. Launch a website that sells the salon

    Chosen +11 · Seen +2

  3. Add categories that match your work

    Add hair extension technician, beauty salon and the other categories that fit, and list every service with its price on your Google profile.

  4. Build a page for each service

    Seen +5 · Chosen +2
