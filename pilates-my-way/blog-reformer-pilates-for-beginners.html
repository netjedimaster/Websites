Reformer Pilates for beginners: your first class, start to finish

What to wear, what happens, and whether you'll keep up. Everything first-timers ask us, answered.

The short answer

Wear fitted workout clothes and grip socks. Arrive 10 minutes early. Your instructor sets up your reformer and explains every move before you do it. It's challenging but adjustable, so beginners keep up. At Pilates My Way, everyone starts in Slow Burn Foundations.

What is a reformer, anyway?

A reformer is a long frame with a sliding carriage, springs underneath, a footbar at one end and straps at the other. You push and pull against the springs while lying, sitting, kneeling or standing on the carriage. The springs can be made lighter or heavier, which is why the same class works for a first-timer and a marathon runner.

It looks intimidating. It isn't, once someone shows you how it works. That's exactly what your first class is for.

What to wear to Pilates

  • Fitted clothes. Leggings or fitted shorts and a top that won't ride up when you're upside down. Loose clothes can catch in the springs.
  • Grip socks. Required at Pilates My Way, and we sell them at the studio.
  • Skip the jewelry and anything with big zippers or buckles.
  • Bring water. You'll want it.

What happens in your first class

  1. Arrive 10 minutes early. We'll show you around and set you up on your reformer.
  2. Learn the machine. Your instructor explains the springs, the straps and the carriage before you start.
  3. Move, one step at a time. Slow, controlled strength and stretch work for your core, posture and whole body.
  4. Leave knowing what's next. How to book your next class and when you're ready to Level Up.

Is Pilates hard?

Yes, and that's the good news. Reformer Pilates moves slowly, which makes your muscles work harder, not easier. You'll feel muscles you didn't know you had. But it's low impact and every move can be scaled, so being out of shape isn't a reason to wait. It's the reason to start.

Most people feel looser and stand taller within the first few weeks. Tone follows with steady classes, usually two or three a week.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience to try reformer Pilates?

No. Slow Burn Foundations is built for people who have never touched a reformer.

What should I wear to reformer Pilates?

Fitted workout clothes and grip socks. Grip socks are required at Pilates My Way and sold at the studio.

Is reformer Pilates hard for beginners?

It's challenging, but every move can be made easier with the springs. Nobody gets left behind.

I have an injury. Can I still start?

Many clients train with modifications. Text us before class so we can plan. If you're under a doctor's care, get clearance first.

How many times a week should a beginner do Pilates?

Two or three classes a week is where most people start to feel the change.
