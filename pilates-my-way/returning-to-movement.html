Finished physical therapy?

Keep getting stronger, safely.

Physical therapy gets you moving again. What comes next matters just as much. Returning to Movement is our small-group reformer class for people rebuilding strength, mobility and confidence after an injury, surgery or time away.

One of our own clients, a physical therapist, wrote that she appreciates "the emphasis on quality movement, body awareness, and proper technique." That's the whole point of our Slow Burn Method: controlled pacing, proper alignment, and modifications for every body.

Questions

Before your first class

I've never done Pilates. Is this for me?

Yes. Your first class is Slow Burn Foundations, with clear, step-by-step coaching on the reformer.

What should I wear?

Comfortable workout clothes. Grip socks are required (we sell them). Bring water and arrive 10 minutes early.

I have an injury. Can I still come?

Yes. Many clients train with modifications. Text us before class so we can plan for you. If you're under a doctor's care, get clearance first.

How long do I have to use my 8 classes?

21 days from purchase.

What are your hours?

Classes run 7 days a week. See the schedule above for times.
