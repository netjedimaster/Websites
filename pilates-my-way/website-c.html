What changes

Move easier. Stand taller. Get toned.

What 2 or 3 classes a week does for your body, at 25 or at 65.

Move easier

Long, controlled stretches on the reformer open up tight hips, hamstrings, shoulders and back. Getting out of the car, bending down, reaching up: it all gets easier.

Stand taller

Deep core and back strength pull your posture back where it belongs. Clients notice it at their desk, in the mirror and in photos.

Get toned

Slow, spring-loaded resistance works every muscle through its full range. You build lean, firm muscle, not bulk, without jumping or pounding your joints.

FAQ

Before your first class

I've never done Pilates. Is this for me?

Yes. Your first class is Slow Burn Foundations, with clear, step-by-step coaching on the reformer.

What should I wear?

Comfortable workout clothes. Grip socks are required (we sell them). Bring water and arrive 10 minutes early.

I have an injury. Can I still come?

Yes. Many clients train with modifications. Text us before class so we can plan for you. If you're under a doctor's care, get clearance first.

How long do I have to use my 8 classes?

21 days from purchase.

What are your hours?

Classes run 7 days a week. See the schedule above for times.
